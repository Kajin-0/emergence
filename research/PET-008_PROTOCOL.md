# PET-008 prospective pilot protocol (frozen before run)

Goal: discriminate early learned feature readout from conventional forecasts of structured held-out modular addition families. Not a claim of discovering an unknown task grammar. Closest baselines: Nanda et al. restricted/excluded logits (arXiv:2301.05217), Sivasankar/Borowiecki et al. Fourier synchronization (arXiv:2606.12966, check current revision and authors). No claim that a lead over grokking alone is novel.

Fixed experiment: p=31; ordered pair inputs represented by independently learned one-hot input weights, 128 hidden square-activation units and linear logits. No prescribed Fourier features. Full-batch AdamW, lr=0.003, weight_decay=1, cross entropy, 2500 updates; CPU, seeds 0,1,2. For each seed choose 20 of 31 differences (b-a mod p) using seeded NumPy permutation; train on all ordered pairs of those differences, withhold 11 complete differences. A family passes at >=90% accuracy across its 31 pairs. Prediction cutoff=600, prior checkpoint=300, horizon=2500. Evaluate only families failing at cutoff. Positive label = pass at horizon; endpoint acquisition not first-passage or persistent grokking.

Freeze these rules: (1) hidden feature ridge classifier trained ONLY on observed train labels at cutoff; center hidden features using train means, scale by train RMS per feature (floor 1e-6), fit ridge lambda=1 to one-hot labels with unpenalized intercept via centering. Predict horizon pass iff ridge accuracy on candidate family >=90%. (2) direct persistence predicts no new pass; rank by cutoff raw accuracy. (3) linear extrapolation of family raw accuracies from 300 to 600 to horizon, clip [0,1], threshold >=90%. (4) task-aware restricted-logit comparator: project full grid logits onto dependence on (a+b) mod p by averaging within sum classes, threshold projected family accuracy >=90%; uses known task symmetry, analogous to but not identical to Nanda frequency filtering. All predictor scores use only cutoff/prior data and fixed modular target grammar; withheld labels allowed to score early baselines and predictions, never fit ridge or learner or tune thresholds. No future information used by predictors. Also report training accuracy/loss at cutoff/horizon and fraction of hidden feature energy explained by sum-class projection (diagnostic, no calibrated forecast). Report per seed eligible count, positives, predicted positives, TP/FP/FN/TN, accuracy, precision/recall when defined, pooled AUC if both classes; family observations within seeds are dependent. Record all outcomes including no-event or all-event cases. No tuning or sweep after observing results; if pilot inconclusive or baseline subsumes, park this specific design. No new spending. This is a minimal exploratory experiment, not independent validation or a semantic capability discovery result.
