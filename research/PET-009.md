## Model and exact scalar clock

Let \(x=(x_1,z)\in\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^{d-1}\), \(f(x)=\sum_{j=1}^N a_j(b_jx_1+u_j^Tz)^2\). All parameters are trained with ordinary Euclidean gradient flow on \(\frac12(f(e_1)-Y)^2\). The training datum is rank one; u_j are frozen because they receive zero gradient. Let \(F=f(e_1)=\sum_j a_j b_j^2\), \(a_j(0)=v_j>0\), \(b_j(0)=\sqrt2 v_j\). Assume \(Y>F(0)\). Gradient equations
\[
\dot a_j=(Y-F)b_j^2,\quad \dot b_j=2(Y-F)a_jb_j,\quad\dot u_j=0.
\]
The balanced relation is invariant (b_j^2-2a_j^2 constant; positive sign persists). Introduce \(s(t)=\int_0^t(Y-F(t'))dt'\). Then
\[
a_j(s)=v_j/(1-2v_js),\quad b_j=\sqrt2 a_j,\quad F(s)=2\sum_j a_j^3,\quad s'=Y-F(s).
\]
Before equilibrium, \(t(s)=\int_0^s du/(Y-F(u))\), strictly increasing, with positive denominators. Since F increases from F(0) to infinity before the first pole, equilibrium clock s_eq is the unique solution F(s_eq)=Y and is reached only at infinite physical time. All future outputs form a quadratic matrix \(Q\):
\[
f((x_1,z),s)=F(s)x_1^2+2x_1 C(s)^Tz+z^TD(s)z,\quad
C(s)=\sqrt2\sum_j a_j(s)^2 u_j,\quad
D(s)=\sum_j a_j(s)u_ju_j^T.
\]
The held-out relations are genuine output differences, e.g. f(e1+e2)-f(e1-e2)=4 C_1. Their targets are specified by the evaluator and not trained. No unknown semantic grammar inferred.

## PET-009A — Arbitrary-order functional and NTK indistinguishability (DERIVED)

For each integer q>=0 choose n=q+4. Take n+1 positive distinct equally spaced nodes \(v_i=(i+1)/(2(n+1)), i=0,\ldots,n\), multiplicities \(m_i={n\choose i}\), width \(N=2^n\). Use two-dimensional input and u=scalar c, model plus uses \(c_i=(-1)^{n-i}/\sqrt2\) for every copy at node i; minus reverses every c. Balanced initialization as above. Training trajectories F(s), the scalar clock, training loss, and all training-input outputs are identical at all times for any common target Y>F(0).

At the initial physical time, the complete functions f(x) and empirical tangent kernels K(x,z) have identical derivatives through order q for every pair of inputs. Proof: common clock s(t) is analytic near 0 with s'(0)>0. The finite difference identity \(\sum_i (-1)^{n-i}m_i v_i^k=0\) for k<n follows from the nth forward difference of a polynomial of degree below n. The only sign-sensitive coefficient of f is C, proportional to \(\sum_i (-1)^{n-i}m_i a_i(s)^2\). Its kth s derivative at zero is proportional to a signed moment of degree 2+k. The complete NTK (all parameters trainable, including c with zero gradient on training datum) is
\[
K(x,z)=\sum_j[(w_j^Tx)^2(w_j^Tz)^2+4a_j^2(w_j^Tx)(w_j^Tz)(x^Tz)],\quad w_j=(\sqrt2 a_j,c_j).
\]
Its terms odd in c contain powers a_j^1 or a_j^3, since c_j^2=1/2 fixed. The kth s derivatives of these coefficients are proportional to signed moments of degree <=3+k, strictly below n for k<=q. All even-c terms agree identically. Composition with the identical analytic s(t) preserves equality of physical-time derivatives through q.

For any positive clock \(0<s<\min(s_{\rm eq},1/(2v_{\max}))\), the held-out relation
\[
R_\pm(s)=f_\pm((1,1),s)-f_\pm((1,-1),s)
=\pm4\sum_i (-1)^{n-i}m_i\frac{v_i^2}{(1-2v_i s)^2}
\]
has strictly positive sign for plus and negative for minus. The nth finite difference of \(g(v)=v^2/(1-2vs)^2\) is positive: use the absolutely convergent series \(g(v)=\sum_{k>=0}(k+1)(2s)^k v^{k+2}\), all coefficients positive and the nth derivative strictly positive for v>0 (n>=4). Equivalently integrate g^(n) over the n-dimensional step cube. Initially R=0 for both. At a fixed horizon s*, choose a functional criterion R>=R_+(s*)/2>0 (or a target R_+(s*) with tolerance smaller than half its positive value). Exactly plus acquires that relation. Choose Y>F(s*) so horizon physical time finite and identical. Thus no fixed temporal derivative order independent of width, even of the full function and full NTK, plus all training-only trajectories, suffices to predict arbitrary held-out functional outcomes in this model class. The two sets of microscopic parameters differ and are identifiable if read directly. This is an observation-limited theorem, NOT an impossibility result with full parameter access. No width-independent output gap: example gap shrinks with q, and width grows exponentially. Neither architecture nor loss learns a new grammar.

## PET-009B — Exact finite-jet reconstruction under bounded spectral support (DERIVED)

Let r be the number of distinct positive initial amplitudes v_j and suppose a bound R>=r is known. Group by amplitude v_i, multiplicity m_i, U_i=sum u_j, V_i=sum u_j u_j^T. Then the entire future function is specified by
\[
F(s)=2\sum_{i=1}^r m_i v_i^3/(1-2v_i s)^3,\quad
C(s)=\sqrt2\sum_i v_i^2 U_i/(1-2v_i s)^2,\quad
D(s)=\sum_i v_i V_i/(1-2v_i s).
\]
The first 2R Taylor coefficients of F, C, D in s (orders 0..2R-1) suffice to recover the grouped state, hence the full future output and scalar clock. Physical-time jets to the same order suffice: s'=Y-F(t), s(0)=0, s'(0)>0, so formal series inversion determines s-to-t coefficients through the required order. Extract Q coefficients from full function or finite quadratic probes, e.g. e_i and e_i+e_j. F coefficients divided by \(2^{k+1}{k+2\choose 2}\) yield moments \(\mu_k=\sum_i m_i v_i^{k+3}\), k=0..2R-1. Positive atom weights ensure the R by R Hankel matrix [mu_(i+j)] has rank r (factorization into Vandermonde times positive diagonal times transpose). Its leading r by r minor is positive definite. Solve \(\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}p_j\mu_{k+j}=-\mu_{k+r}\), k=0..r-1. The monic annihilating polynomial has precisely v_i as roots; then Vandermonde solve gives weights and multiplicities. From first r coefficients of C and D, rescale by \(\sqrt2 (k+1)2^k\) and \(2^k\) respectively, then solve on same nodes for \(v_i^2U_i\) and \(v_iV_i\). This recovers all grouped data exactly. State size O(r d^2), compared to O(Nd) parameters, so a size reduction is possible when N>>r d. The procedure uses classical Prony reconstruction; exact arithmetic, sufficiently informative output derivatives, known model restrictions assumed. Derivative extraction, root conditioning, scalar quadrature, acquisition of observations and precision costs are not bounded here. No claim of a practical or generic learning speedup. May be ill-conditioned for close nodes. If full initial parameters are available, group them directly instead of inferring via moments.

## PET-009C — Width-independent approximate output certificate without Prony (DERIVED)

In the same model assume v_j<=A known, choose s_max>0 with zeta=2A s_max<1. Let F_K,C_K,D_K be Taylor truncations in s of degree K, computed from exact initial functional derivatives using the clock conversion. Define \(B=\operatorname{tr}D(0)=\sum v_j||u_j||^2\), \(F_0=F(0)\). By Cauchy-Schwarz \(\sqrt2\sum v_j^2||u_j||\le\sqrt{F_0 B}\). For 0<=s<=s_max, the geometric-series expansions give uniform bounds
\[
0\le F-F_K\le E_F=F_0\sum_{k>K}{k+2\choose2}\zeta^k,
\quad ||C-C_K||\le E_C=\sqrt{F_0B}\sum_{k>K}(k+1)\zeta^k,
\quad ||D-D_K||_{op}\le E_D=B\zeta^{K+1}/(1-\zeta).
\]
Thus \(||Q-Q_K||_{op}\le E_Q=E_F+2E_C+E_D\). These sums converge geometrically for fixed zeta<1, with polynomial-in-K prefactors. They are known without discovering individual amplitudes. Suppose \(Y-F_K(s_{\max})-E_F>0\), which certifies positivity of the actual residual through s_max. Approximate clock \(\hat s'=Y-F_K(\hat s), \hat s(0)=0\). For \(T\le\int_0^{s_{\max}}du/(Y-F_K(u))\) both clocks are in domain, actual s<=hat s by comparison. Because F_K increasing (positive coefficients), \((\hat s-s)'\le E_F\), hence clock error <=T E_F. A uniform derivative bound for Q is
\[
L_Q=\frac{6A F_0}{(1-\zeta)^4}+\frac{8A\sqrt{F_0 B}}{(1-\zeta)^3}+\frac{2AB}{(1-\zeta)^2}.
\]
Derive by differentiating the rational functions and applying same initial bounds. Therefore
\[
|f(x,T)-x^TQ_K(\hat s(T))x|
\le ||x||^2 (E_Q+L_Q T E_F).
\]
This yields a uniform functional output certificate over every unit-norm input (and finite linear combinations via triangle inequality). If the predicted value or relation lies farther from its threshold than its error bound, certify future passing/failing; otherwise abstain. No root recovery, and O(K d^2) coefficient storage independent of width, conditional on supplied exact derivative data, known A, initial B and clock-domain margin. For fixed A,s_max,T,F0,B and absolute tolerance, K grows logarithmically in inverse tolerance up to log-log terms. Relative errors, noisy derivative extraction and large-width scaling of F0/B may destroy usefulness. At late times near a pole or equilibrium the margin/domain condition becomes difficult; this is not a universal horizon or complexity bound. No comparison of measured runtime against frozen NTK, direct training or sampling. This result is a constructive way to overcome PET-009A at prescribed accuracy, not contradiction of exact nonidentifiability.
