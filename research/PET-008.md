## Conclusion

Reject the frozen hypothesis as a reliable predictive advantage in this pilot. Decodability is not a sufficient indicator of future expressed behavior. The study is consistent with prior work on latent generalization mechanisms, but shows a gap between a task being expressible by early features and the actual optimizer selecting/exhibiting it. This is empirical evidence in a narrow model, not a universal impossibility theorem. Do not optimize the threshold, seeds, checkpoint, architecture or training split retrospectively; park this design. No publication-grade novelty established.
