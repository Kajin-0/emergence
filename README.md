# Predictive Emergence Theory (PET)

Working mathematical research repository — initialized 2026-11-23 ET (2026-11-24 UTC).

**Question:** Given the microscopic dynamics and initial state of a complex adaptive system, can one discover and predict previously unspecified emergent structures before observing them, using materially less information or computation than full simulation?

This is an exploratory research program, not an established general theory of intelligence. Current results concern explicit mathematical models. No publication-grade originality or real AI capability-discovery result has been established.

## Current frontier

PET-004: mixed stable/unstable spectral systems. Investigate finite-information prediction of exit timing and limiting structure without assuming analytic linearization or a convergent finite normal form. Compare against invariant-manifold, normal-form and Dulac-transition theory before claiming novelty. Global sequences of transitions and discovery of semantic capabilities remain longer-term objectives.

Constraints: $0 new spending; theory first; high significance threshold; no publication or priority claims without independent scrutiny and owner approval. Public repository is a working record.
